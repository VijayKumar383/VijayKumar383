My primary responsibility is ensuring that the numbers presented to leadership are accurate, traceable, and defensible — and that discrepancies across systems are identified, explained, and resolved before they influence business decisions.

How I Work

My approach is shaped by production analytics in regulated, high-impact domains:
	1.	Traceability over Assumption
Every metric must be traceable from executive view to curated layer to source system.
	2.	Reconciliation before Visualization
Dashboards are only as valuable as the validation behind them. I reconcile first, visualize second.
	3.	Contracts, not Just Queries
KPIs are treated as contracts: fixed definitions, tested logic, and documented lineage.
	4.	Explainability for Leadership
Variance and trend movement must be explainable in business terms, not only technical ones.
